# Moving focus from knowledge base to agent platform

[Story selector](../story-bank.md) · [Gaps](../gaps.md)

**Use for:** Competing priorities and handing over owned work. First draft ready for rehearsal, not rehearsed. Use the separate SDK-priority story for supporting a decision you opposed.

## Facts and answer structure

1. You had owned knowledge-base design, implementation, improvement and adoption.
2. The team judged AI platform work more important and stopped its own work on the knowledge base.
3. The knowledge base was handed over to another team while your team focused on improving the platform.
4. The reasoning was that platform progress needed to accelerate because the knowledge-base use case would be less relevant without it. This is the team’s prioritization rationale, not a claim that the knowledge base had no existing uses.
5. You directed the receiving team to documentation you had written and worked with them on their first few PRs to support understanding of the problem and solution.
6. Long-term ownership outcomes and quantified impact of the reprioritization remain unspecified.

## Spoken draft

> We decided to prioritize accelerating the AI platform over continuing our work on the knowledge base. Our reasoning was that the knowledge-base use case would be less relevant without progress on the platform.
>
> We handed the knowledge base over to another team. I pointed them to the documentation I had written and worked with them on their first few PRs to help them understand both the problem and the solution. That let our team focus on improving the platform while transferring the knowledge-base work.

## Frame for the question

This supplies the actual sacrifice missing from the earlier initiative story: your team relinquished further work on a project you had built. The dependency rationale explains why the competing work mattered more, and documentation plus initial PR collaboration establishes your contribution. A handover is a concrete action; continuity and successful long-term ownership still require evidence.

Culture connection: prioritizing the wider usefulness of the work and helping the receiving team take it on. Do not invent reluctance, disagreement, personal decision authority or a seamless transition.

## Optional rehearsal detail

How did the receiving team operate afterward, and what platform work did the freed capacity enable? Do not claim measured acceleration or a seamless handover without supporting facts.
