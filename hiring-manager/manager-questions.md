# Your selected questions for the manager

[Preparation overview](README.md)

## First 90 days

“What would you want me to understand, own and deliver in my first 90 days, and how would you judge whether I was on track?”

## Recent layoffs and team direction

“I saw the recent layoff announcement. How has it affected this team's staffing and priorities, and has it changed the scope or expectations of this role?”

Useful follow-up: “What makes this role a priority investment now?” Ask about concrete team impact and the reason for hiring rather than requesting a guarantee about future cuts.

AP reported the announcement on 14 October 2026, including impacts to product and technology among other functions. That does not establish an effect on this particular team. [AP report](https://apnews.com/article/2290c8472a155adb9c4b85fdc885bc7e), checked 19 October 2026.

## Hybrid working

“What does the team's working week look like in practice: which days do people work together in the office, how much flexibility is there, and are there regular meetings across time zones?”

These are legitimate questions about the job. The exact team schedule remains unknown; ask the manager about practice and the recruiter about any formal policy that needs confirmation.
