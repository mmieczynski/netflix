Scope and boundaries
- These are original educational exercises, not confirmed Netflix interview
  questions or a description of Netflix's actual recommendation algorithms.
- No fixed reading or conversation duration is promised.
- The book is usable independently without an editor or running code. Spoken
  work does not establish Go syntax fluency or provide compilation evidence.
- Inputs and arithmetic bounds, clock monotonicity, callback order, concurrency,
  and retention assumptions are stated beside the relevant examples.
- GitHub changes are committed directly to main as requested; updating the book
  does not mark study topics as learned.
